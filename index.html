Our little story

Arsorel began as a pair of hands, a bundle of pipe cleaners, and a simple wish — to make flowers that could be kept forever. Every piece we make is hand-carved, hand-twisted and hand-made, so no two are ever exactly alike. From single-flower bouquets to tiny keychains and cosy home décor, each treasure is crafted slowly and with a lot of love, then sent out into the world to be cherished.
